# Brain Gym roadmap

Ideas discussed with Nick, roughly in priority order. Move items to NOTES.md when built.

## Waiting on Nick
- Family photos, with captions (who + roughly when).
- More personal routines: what he does and what goes wrong.
- Confirm the fire setup (wood stove? fireplace?) and the stove (coils or flat glass, knob layout).
- His interests (hobbies, old work, teams) to theme the games so they feel like his.

## Next to build
- **Personal bests** on every exercise ("New best: 88%, up from 81%").
- **Read the face:** pick the feeling (happy, worried, annoyed, joking), later using family photos. Targets right-brain emotion reading.
- **Remote progress for Nick:** scores are iPad-only today. Could reuse the `api.php` pattern to sync a summary.
- **Notes from family:** Nick types a message that shows when Dad opens the app.
- **Finish line:** end a workout with a voice message, a favorite song, or a weekly recap.

## Later exercises (Lumosity-style)
- **Which way?** Flanker task: swipe the direction the middle fish faces, with flocks often on the left.
- **Train switch:** route colored trains entering from the left. Divided attention. Hard, so save for later.
- **Making change:** price plus payment, pick the change. Practical money practice.
- **Simple screw puzzle:** a gentle Screwdom-like game with big screws, no ads, tracking left vs. right misses.

## Family games
- Built: Photo Match (local + online).
- **Hide and Seek:** shared scene, take turns hiding an object and giving spoken clues. Left-side scanning plus following directions.
- **Who's That?** family photo revealed tile by tile, race to guess who, where, when.
- **Say It Like You Mean It:** say a line with a secret emotion, others guess it. Tone and sarcasm work.
- **Family Expert:** trivia about the family's own history, with Dad as the expert.

## Known limits
- Brain-training gains mostly stay in the game. Real-task practice (routines, reaching, socializing) matters more, so keep the app pointed there.
- Have his OT or speech therapist review which exercises and levels fit.
